Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Viralizer.ai provides tools for discovering content opportunities, creating videos, and publishing user-approved videos to connected platforms. This policy explains the information we process when you use Viralizer Video Studio and Creatorthon.

Information we collect

  • Google account identity information used to sign you in, such as your name and email address.
  • Content you submit or create, including topics, prompts, narration, logos, generated videos, captions, and hashtags.
  • Project, usage, and technical records needed to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the service.
  • When you connect YouTube, an authorization token that permits the uploads you request. Viralizer does not receive your Google password.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, provide content intelligence, generate and store requested media, publish videos only after a user initiates publishing, enforce event limits, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.

Google and YouTube data

Viralizer requests the minimum Google permission needed to upload a video to the YouTube account selected by the user. The connected account token is encrypted at rest and is used only for user-requested publishing. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Storage, sharing, and retention

We use service providers for hosting, databases, object storage, video generation, speech generation, and publishing. Information is shared with these processors only as needed to deliver the requested service. Projects and authorization records are retained while needed to provide the service, comply with law, resolve disputes, and protect the platform. Users can delete projects through their workspace and disconnect YouTube at any time.

Your choices

You may disconnect YouTube in Viralizer and revoke Google access from your Google Account permissions. You may also request access to or deletion of your account information by contacting us.
